Why Kenya & why Watamu

Some places you visit.
Others you feel.

Kenya is one of those places you should experience at least once in your lifetime — where the wind, the ocean, the wildlife and the endless savannah remind you what freedom feels like. On the coast, in Watamu, the rhythm of life slows down: barefoot days, warm ocean water, wild landscapes and a deep connection with nature.

Come for the adventure. Stay for the feeling.

02

Wind

Two trade winds.
Wind most of the year.

Watamu sits between two monsoon trade winds: the warm, steady Kaskazi from the north-east and the stronger, more consistent Kusi from the south-east.

15+knots
  • North-east trade wind
  • Warm & steady conditions
  • Ideal for lessons, progression and long kitesurf days
20+knots
  • South-east trade wind
  • Stronger and more consistent wind
  • Great for progression and experienced riders
Variable
  • Variable winds, lighter and less consistent
  • Some rainfall, especially in April – May
  • Good days still possible, with more varied conditions

    Meet Federica

    The person behind Marea.

    I’m Federica, a kitesurf instructor who has been working in the kite industry for the past seven years — in kite schools, organising lessons, managing guests and creating unforgettable experiences on and off the water.

    I understand what it takes to organise a successful camp: good logistics, flexibility, reliable local support and a welcoming place where everyone can feel at home.

    I’ve been living in Watamu for many years, and this place has become a big part of who I am. What I love most is the way it brings us closer to nature and gives us a true sense of freedom. Life here moves at a different pace, reminding us that happiness can be found in simple things.

    That’s how MAREA was born — a place that brings together my experience, my love for Watamu and my desire to share this lifestyle with others. A home base where kite schools, instructors and groups can focus on what they love, while we take care of the rest.

    — Federica
